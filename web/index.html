    About this notebook

    Save English words with their definition and Sinhala meaning. The card at the top rotates through them so you keep meeting words you've saved.

    Keep it on your home screen

    1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.
    2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
    3. It gets its own icon and opens straight to a word.

    A web page can't draw a live iOS widget tile — only a native app can. This is the closest equivalent: one tap, and a word is in front of you.

    Study mode

    Turn on Study and the meaning stays hidden until you tap the card. Rotation pauses so you can think.

    Keyboard

    ← and → move through the deck, S shuffles, N adds a word.
