    About this notebook

    Save English words with their definition and Sinhala meaning. The card at the top rotates through them so you keep meeting words you've saved.

    Keep it on your home screen

    1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.
    2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
    3. It gets its own icon, opens fullscreen with no browser bars, and works with no signal.

    A web page can't draw a live iOS widget tile — only a native app can. This is the closest equivalent: one tap, and a word is in front of you.

    Where your words live

    On this device, in this browser — nothing is sent anywhere. That also means they don't follow you to another phone, and clearing website data erases them. Export writes them to a file you can keep; Import reads one back, merging by word rather than replacing.

    Filling a word in

    Type the English word and the definition, Sinhala meaning and example sentences are looked up for you. Suggested fields are tinted — check them before saving, and edit anything that reads wrong. A suggestion never overwrites text you typed yourself.

    The translation is machine-made and free, so it is sometimes unavailable and sometimes just off. The line under the word field tells you what came back; whatever is missing, type it in.

    Study mode

    Turn on Study and the meaning stays hidden until you tap the card. Rotation pauses so you can think.

    Keyboard

    ← and → move through the deck, S shuffles, N adds a word.
